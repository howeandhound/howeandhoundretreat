About Me

Hi, I’m Alex.

Howe & Hound started because I struggled to find somewhere I truly trusted with my own dogs.

All three of mine are rescues, and each came to me with their own story. Bandit was pulled from Tennessee after being found in a culvert in a near-death situation, and I knew from the beginning that dogs deserved a different kind of care.

When I traveled, I didn’t want to simply drop my dogs off at a kennel. I wanted someone who would know them, understand their quirks, give them plenty of time outside, and care for them with patience and dignity.

So I created that place.

Howe & Hound is a small, personal boarding experience on 11 beautiful acres, built around giving every dog the attention, space, and care I would want for my own.

This is much deeper than a typical boarding setup, because every stay helps me help dogs like Bandit get a second chance at life.
