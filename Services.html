Day Camp

Howe & Hound Day Camp is designed for dogs who need more than a few hours in a crowded playgroup. Days are intentionally kept small and personalized, with plenty of outdoor time, structure, and one-on-one attention.

Whether your dog needs an adventure day, extra exercise while you’re at work, socialization, or simply somewhere safe to spend the day, their experience is tailored to their personality and routine.

Because every dog and schedule is different, Day Camp pricing is customized. Contact us to build the right plan for your dog.

Boarding

Boarding at Howe & Hound is intentionally small-scale so every dog gets the time and attention they deserve. Guests enjoy a comfortable place to settle in, plenty of outdoor time on our property, and a schedule that supports rest, play, and routine.

We take the time to get to know each dog, their routine, personality, quirks, comfort level, and what makes them happiest. Whether that means an active day outside or a quieter stay with extra reassurance, we adapt to the dog in front of us.

Pick up and drop off service is available upon request.

Boarding rates are customized based on your dog’s stay and individual needs. Contact us for personalized pricing and availability.

My routine and my promise to you

Effective spring-fall, winter is full time care.

Full transparency about my routine: I work during the day on weekdays. Your dog gets two hours of active time with me before I leave — potty breaks, play, training, and a chance to settle in and decompress.

I am committed to helping your dog feel safe, stimulated, and cared for in a way that fits their individual needs.
