Services

Day Camp

More than daycare — a day built around your dog.

Howe & Hound Day Camp is designed for dogs who need more than a few hours in a crowded playgroup. Days are intentionally kept small and personalized, with plenty of outdoor time, structured play, and calm, consistent care.

Whether your dog needs an adventure day, extra exercise while you’re at work, socialization, or simply somewhere safe to spend the day, their experience is tailored to their personality, energy, and comfort level.

Because every dog and schedule is different, Day Camp pricing is customized. Contact us to build the right plan for your dog.

Boarding

A stay that feels a little less like boarding and a lot more like a country retreat.

Boarding at Howe & Hound is intentionally small-scale so every dog gets the time and attention they deserve. Guests enjoy a comfortable place to settle in, plenty of outdoor time on our country property, and a routine built around trust, enrichment, and calm companionship.

We take the time to get to know each dog, their routine, personality, quirks, comfort level, and what makes them happiest. Whether that means an active day outside or a quieter stay with more one-on-one time, every stay is tailored to the dog in front of us.

Pick up and drop off service available upon request.

Boarding rates are customized based on your dog’s stay and individual needs. Contact us for personalized pricing and availability.
